# Instagram Ad Specs

Character limits, sizes and best practices for ads on Instagram, bought through
Meta. Verify in Ads Manager's preview before launch, because limits and
interface overlays change.

---

## Single image, video and carousel

| Element | Recommended | Maximum | Notes |
|---------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Primary text | 125 chars | 2,200 chars | Text above or under the media; truncated after about 125 |
| Headline | 40 chars | 255 chars | Below the media; truncated after about 40 |
| Description | 30 chars | 255 chars | Below the headline; may not show |
| URL display link | 40 chars | n/a | Optional custom display URL |

## Placement-specific notes

- **Feed:** all elements can show; primary text is most visible. Image
  1080x1350 (4:5) uses the most screen; 1080x1080 (1:1) is safe.
- **Reels:** vertical 1080x1920 (9:16). Primary text is overlaid; keep it
  under about 72 characters. Captions burned in, hook in the first second.
- **Stories:** vertical 1080x1920 (9:16). The top and bottom bands are covered
  by the profile bar and the reply or CTA bar. Keep faces, text and the key
  visual out of those bands.
- **Explore and profile feed:** treat like Feed.
- **Carousel:** 2 to 10 cards. 1080x1080 or 1080x1350 per card. The first card
  is the ad. Build the cover to earn the swipe (`/ig-carousel`).

Safe-zone worst case for vertical video (cross-platform) is the 720x1200 text
band, detailed in `short-form-video-specs.md`.

## Best practices

- Front-load the hook in the first 125 characters of primary text.
- Use line breaks for readability in longer primary text.
- Emojis: use only if the account already does, one or two at most.
- Questions in primary text can increase engagement.
- Headline should be a clear action or value statement.
- Keep the image text short and inside the safe zone.
- Native-looking creative (phone-shot, captioned, spoken to one person)
  outperforms the polished-ad look.

## Lead ads (instant form)

| Element | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| Greeting headline | 60 chars |
| Greeting description | 360 chars |
| Privacy policy text | 200 chars |

## Click-to-DM ads

The creative is a normal Feed, Reels or Stories ad. The CTA opens a thread.
Write the welcome message and quick replies as part of the creative
(`/ig-dm`), and keep the response-time promise in the ad honest.

## Partnership ads

Run from the creator's handle. Caption and creative are the creator's, with
the brand's paid partnership label. Keep the creator's voice. Ask for
usage and paid-amplification rights in writing before launch.

## Character counting tips

- Spaces count as characters.
- Emojis count as 1 to 2 characters.
- Special characters (|, &, #) count as 1.
- URLs in body text count against limits.
- Always verify in the ad preview before launching.

## Multi-placement adaptation

Start with the most restrictive format and expand:

1. **Reels and Stories text** (about 72 chars): forces the tightest message
2. **Headline** (40 chars): add a word or two
3. **Feed primary text** (125 chars visible): add context and proof
4. **Long caption** (up to 2,200): the full story, for the reader who taps "more"

The core message then works in every placement, and gets richer where there is
room.
