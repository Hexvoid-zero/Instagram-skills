# Creator and Collab PR

How to earn features and collaborations with creators and accounts on
Instagram, as a brand or as a creator.

## Choose the right partners

Audience fit beats follower count. A creator with 8,000 engaged followers in
your niche beats one with 400,000 who do not care.

Score each prospect on:

| Signal | Check |
|---|---|
| Audience overlap | Do their followers match your buyer? Ask for demographics |
| Content quality | Do their posts hold attention? Compare reach to followers |
| Engagement | Real comments, saves and shares, not a sea of emoji |
| Brand safety | Scan recent posts and comments |
| Prior brand work | Do paid posts look natural? Do they disclose? |
| Reachability | A contact in bio, or a manager |

Red flags: sudden follower jumps, comment sections full of generic praise,
reach far below follower count, refusal to share insights.

## Formats

| Format | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Gifting (no obligation)** | Testing fit with micro-creators. A thoughtful item, a plain note, no script |
| **Collab post** | Co-authored post visible on both profiles. Good for reach and follows |
| **Paid creator content** | A defined deliverable with usage terms |
| **Takeover or Live** | Shared audience, one day, clear topic |
| **Affiliate or code** | Trackable sales |
| **Partnership ad** | Run the creator's post as an ad. See `/ig-ads` |
| **Retainer series** | A recurring format with one creator |

## The outreach message

Keep it short and specific. Write it with `/ig-dm` and run `/ig-human`.

```
Hi [name], I have followed [specific post] for a while. [one honest line why].
I run [brand/account], [one line]. I would like to [specific idea]: [format,
deliverable, timing]. If it is a fit, I can share [terms/product]. If not,
no problem. Best way to reach you?
```

Rules: no mass-copied template, one clear ask, a reason it suits them, and an
easy no.

## Terms to settle in writing

- Deliverables, dates, formats
- Approval process and number of revisions
- Usage rights and duration (organic, paid amplification, website)
- Exclusivity
- Payment, timing, and what happens if the content does not run
- Disclosure wording and the paid partnership label
- Who owns the content and the raw files
- Reporting: insights the creator will share after posting

A person with authority signs contracts. Do not let an agent or a script
negotiate terms.

## Disclosure

Gifted and paid content must be disclosed as the platform and local law
require. Use the paid partnership label and clear wording. Do not ask a creator
to hide a relationship. Do not present a paid post as spontaneous.

## Measuring a collab

Per collab, record: reach, saves, shares, follows gained, link taps, code
redemptions, DMs, and sales, with the UTM or code that ties each back. Keep
results in `log.md`. A collab that pays off once is a lead, not a strategy.
Repeat it before scaling.

## What not to do

- Mass-gift 500 accounts hoping for posts
- Buy followers or engagement
- Write the creator's script word for word
- Ignore a bad fit because the number is big
- Pay more than you can measure
