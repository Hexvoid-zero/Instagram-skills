# 70 Instagram Marketing Ideas

Each idea has a one-line description and a first step. Numbers match the table
in `SKILL.md`.

## Contents

- Reels reach (1-10)
- Carousels and saves (11-17)
- Stories and community (18-26)
- Comments and DMs (27-34)
- Collabs and creators (35-44)
- Profile and conversion (45-52)
- Launches and offers (53-60)
- Paid (61-66)
- Offline and guerrilla (67-70)

## Reels reach (1-10)

1. **Signature series.** A named, repeatable Reel format with a fixed opening
   and a reason to follow the next one. First step: pick the one format in `log.md`
   that beat the median twice and name it.
2. **Niche problem, one clip.** One painfully specific problem solved in under
   30 seconds. First step: list five problems from DMs and comments, film the
   most common.
3. **Trend with a niche twist.** Reuse a trending audio or format with a
   point of view only your audience gets. First step: check `/ig-viral` for
   what is rising, pick one that fits your voice.
4. **Reply-with-Reel.** Answer a comment or question as a video. First step:
   pick the best comment from `/ig-reply`'s list.
5. **Before and after, shown not told.** Proof as the hook. First step: pull
   three real transformations you have permission to show.
6. **Teardown Reel.** Break down a known post, product, site or result and say
   why it works. First step: choose a subject your audience already looks at.
7. **Myth in the first line.** Open on a belief the niche holds and disprove it
   with evidence. First step: write five myths, keep the one you can back.
8. **Behind the scenes of the work.** The process people never see. First step:
   film the next real task for 20 seconds.
9. **Founder-on-camera authority.** Face and voice, one clear opinion per Reel.
   First step: script three opinions with `/ig-reel` and film in one session.
10. **Reel factory.** Batch 8 to 12 Reels in one day from a topic bank. First
    step: block four hours and prepare the hooks first.

## Carousels and saves (11-17)

11. **Checklist carousel.** A practical list worth saving. First step: turn the
    most-asked question into a 7-slide checklist.
12. **Framework carousel.** A named model with a diagram. First step: name the
    method you already use with clients.
13. **Teardown carousel.** Annotated screenshots and a verdict. First step: pick
    one example and annotate it.
14. **Mistakes carousel.** "Seven mistakes" in your niche, ranked. First step:
    use real DM questions as the mistakes.
15. **Story carousel.** A narrative in 8 slides with a payoff. First step:
    choose a client or personal story with a clear before and after.
16. **Cheat sheet carousel.** Reference material people will screenshot. First
    step: list the numbers or terms the audience keeps asking about.
17. **Reel to carousel repurpose.** Turn a winning Reel into a saved carousel.
    First step: run `/ig-repurpose` on your best Reel.

## Stories and community (18-26)

18. **Question box of the week.** One sticker, one answer thread. First step:
    post it on a fixed day and save the answers.
19. **Poll to product.** Use polls to choose what you build or post. First
    step: put two real options in a poll this week.
20. **Daily Story rhythm.** A short, consistent sequence with a repeating
    structure. First step: choose three recurring slots.
21. **Customer Story reposts.** Reshare real use of your product with
    permission. First step: set up a saved tag folder.
22. **Close-friends list for buyers.** A private list with early access and
    behind-the-scenes. First step: invite the ten most engaged customers.
23. **Story link sticker per post.** Point traffic with a trackable link.
    First step: give each link its own UTM.
24. **Countdown sticker.** Build anticipation for a date. First step: attach it
    to the next real deadline.
25. **Story quiz.** A short quiz that sorts viewers into segments. First step:
    write three questions with segment-specific follow-ups.
26. **Highlights as FAQ.** Objections answered in short Stories, always on.
    First step: list the five questions that block purchase.

## Comments and DMs (27-34)

27. **Pillar-account commenting.** Thoughtful comments on ten larger accounts
    in the niche, daily. First step: pick the ten with `/ig-comment`.
28. **Comment-to-DM keyword.** "Comment GUIDE and I'll send it." First step:
    write the keyword, the auto-reply and the first message with `/ig-dm`.
29. **Pinned comment with the next step.** Seed the conversation and the link.
    First step: pin one useful comment on every Reel.
30. **DM nurture sequence.** Three messages after a keyword trigger, each
    helpful. First step: draft them in your voice.
31. **Welcome message.** The first thing a new DM thread shows. First step:
    set it up with quick replies.
32. **Voice-note replies.** Short voice notes to warm leads. First step: send
    five this week.
33. **Reply sweep.** A daily 15-minute pass on comments and DMs. First step:
    use `/ig-reply` and schedule the slot.
34. **Comment mining.** Turn recurring comments into post topics and ads.
    First step: tag the top ten comments by theme.

## Collabs and creators (35-44)

35. **Collab posts.** Co-authored posts that appear on both grids. First
    step: invite one adjacent account with a complementary audience.
36. **Account swap takeover.** A day of each other's Stories. First step: agree
    the topic and the measurement.
37. **Micro-creator gifting.** Send product to 20 creators with real audiences
    in the niche and no script. First step: build the list and write a plain
    ask.
38. **Paid creator series.** Retainer with one creator for a recurring format.
    First step: pick the creator whose organic content already moves your
    audience.
39. **Partnership ads.** Run the creator's post as an ad with the paid
    partnership label. First step: get usage and amplification rights in
    writing.
40. **Affiliate codes.** Trackable codes per creator. First step: set a code
    and a commission rule.
41. **Expert interviews.** Reel or Live with a recognized name. First step:
    list five people your audience trusts.
42. **Customer co-creation.** Feature a customer's story as a series. First
    step: ask three happy customers.
43. **Instagram Live with a guest.** A scheduled conversation with a clear
    topic. First step: set a date and promote it for a week.
44. **Brand + brand collab.** Shared offer or giveaway with a non-competing
    brand. First step: list complementary brands.

## Profile and conversion (45-52)

45. **Bio rewrite for the one visitor.** Who it is for, what they get, next
    step. First step: run `/ig-profile`.
46. **Pinned posts as the pitch.** Three pins: best proof, best explainer,
    best offer. First step: choose from `log.md`.
47. **Highlights as funnel.** Start here, Proof, FAQ, Offer. First step:
    rebuild covers and order.
48. **Link-in-bio per post.** Per-content tracking. First step: use a tool that
    reports clicks per link.
49. **Lead magnet.** A short asset in exchange for an email or DM. First step:
    turn your top-saved carousel into the asset.
50. **Booking link CTA.** For services, one clear path. First step: put the
    calendar link in the bio and Story.
51. **Social proof wall.** Testimonial Highlight and recurring proof posts.
    First step: collect ten quotes with permission.
52. **Shop and product tags.** For physical products. First step: tag the top
    three sellers in posts.

## Launches and offers (53-60)

53. **Waitlist teaser.** Build demand before the product exists. First step:
    one Reel and a sticker link.
54. **Behind-the-launch series.** Document the build in public. First step:
    plan five episodes.
55. **Challenge.** A 5 to 7 day participation event with a hashtag. First step:
    write the daily prompt and the finish line.
56. **Giveaway done right.** Prize relevant to the niche, simple rules,
    entry by comment or share. First step: check local rules and platform
    guidelines before announcing.
57. **Limited drop.** Real scarcity with a date. First step: set a real cap.
58. **Launch countdown Stories.** Three days of Stories to an opening. First
    step: script each day.
59. **Founder letter post.** A long caption telling the why. First step: write
    it with `/ig-caption`.
60. **Post-launch case study.** Publish what happened with numbers. First
    step: record the metrics from day one.

## Paid (61-66)

61. **Boost the winner.** Put budget behind a post already beating the median.
    First step: choose it from `log.md`.
62. **Retargeting engagers.** Four-ad sequence to people who watched or saved.
    First step: build the audience, see `/ig-ads`.
63. **Click-to-DM ad.** A conversation as the conversion. First step: set the
    welcome message and response promise.
64. **Creator whitelisting.** Run through a creator's handle. First step:
    request partnership permission.
65. **Lookalike from buyers.** Prospect from the people who bought. First step:
    upload a clean customer list.
66. **Always-on creative testing.** A steady stream of variants. First step:
    set a weekly batch with `/ig-ad-creative`.

## Offline and guerrilla (67-70)

67. **QR moment.** A scannable code in a physical place that leads to a
    specific Reel or offer. First step: pick a place your audience actually is.
68. **Packaging insert.** A card that earns a tag, a review or a follow. First
    step: give a reason, not just a handle.
69. **Local stunt.** A visible, story-worthy act in your area, filmed. First
    step: pick one with a clear shareable moment.
70. **Event content.** Turn every event, class or pop-up into a Reel series.
    First step: assign a filmer before the day.
