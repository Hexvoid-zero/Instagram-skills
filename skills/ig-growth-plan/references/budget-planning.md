# Budget Planning

Size the plan to what the account can afford in hours and money.

## Hours first

Most Instagram budgets are time. List weekly hours by task:

| Task | Typical weekly hours |
|---|---|
| Strategy and planning | 1 |
| Filming and editing Reels | 3 to 6 |
| Carousels and Stories | 2 to 3 |
| Community (replies, DMs, commenting) | 3 to 5 |
| Reporting and review | 1 |

If the owner has 6 hours, the plan has 6 hours. Cut formats, not sleep.

## Money

Common lines: editing help, design tools, creator gifting and fees, giveaway
prizes, ad spend, link and DM tools, equipment.

| Stage | Reasonable starting split |
|---|---|
| Foundations | Time only, plus tools |
| Engine building | Editing help, creator seeding, small boosts |
| Scaling | Creator retainers, partnership ads, structured paid |

## Paid sizing

Start only after organic has found something worth amplifying. Begin with a
test budget the account can lose in full. Use `/ig-ads` for the 80/20 rule,
caps and scale rules. Judge spend against payback period, not clicks.

## Rules

- Never fund paid to rescue a conversion path that is broken.
- Put a cap and a review date on every line.
- Reserve a small share for testing so the plan can learn.
