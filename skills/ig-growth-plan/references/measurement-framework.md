# Measurement Framework

Measure to decide, not to decorate. Pick three to five metrics, set the
baseline, review on a cadence.

## Metrics by stage

| Stage | Metric | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Non-follower reach share, follows per Reel, shares per reach | Compare to the account's own median |
| Activation | Profile visits to link taps, DMs started, lead magnet claims | Needs UTM per post |
| Revenue | DM-to-sale rate, bookings, orders, revenue per 1,000 reached | Attribute with codes and "how did you hear" |
| Retention | Story reach and replies, repeat purchases | Close-friends engagement |
| Referral | Shares, tags, branded mentions, codes redeemed | |

Ignore likes as a headline metric. Saves and shares show value. Watch time and
completion show whether the hook held.

## Baselines

Use the last 30 to 60 posts. The yardstick is the **account's own median**, not
an industry benchmark. A post is a winner when it beats the median by a stated
margin, such as 2x reach or 2x saves.

## Review cadence

- **Weekly:** reach, follows, DMs, link taps. Route movers to the owning loop.
- **Monthly:** post-mortem on formats, hooks and pillars. Propose Learnings.
- **Quarterly:** re-plan. Score the rubric again.

## Hygiene

- UTM on every link, a different one per post where possible.
- A DM log with outcome per lead: asked, qualified, booked, bought.
- Export insights monthly so history is not lost.
- Do not read trends from fewer than 10 posts.
