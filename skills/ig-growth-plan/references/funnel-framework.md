# The Instagram Funnel (AARRR)

AARRR is a way to find the leak, not a rigid ladder. Score each stage, fix the
worst one first.

## Acquisition: strangers reach and follow

- **Levers:** Reels reach, collabs, pillar-account commenting, hashtags and
  search keywords in captions, shares, Explore.
- **Signals:** non-follower reach share, follows per Reel, shares per reach.
- **Failure modes:** reach with no follows (unclear who it is for), follows
  with no fit (broad hooks), one-hit viral with no series behind it.

## Activation: a follower takes a first step

- **Levers:** bio and pinned posts, Highlights, Story link stickers, comment
  keyword to DM, lead magnet, a clear next step in every Reel.
- **Signals:** profile visits to link taps, DMs started, lead magnet claims.
- **Failure modes:** no obvious next step, link with no tracking, DM replies
  that arrive a day late.

## Revenue: a step becomes money

- **Levers:** offer clarity, proof, DM sales conversation, booking page, shop
  tags, launches, retargeting.
- **Signals:** DMs to sale, bookings, orders per 1,000 reached, revenue per post.
- **Failure modes:** attention without an offer, a price that does not match the
  audience, no follow-up on warm leads.

## Retention: buyers and followers stay

- **Levers:** close-friends list, Stories rhythm, community replies, customer
  features, post-purchase content.
- **Signals:** Story reach and replies, repeat purchase, saves per follower.
- **Failure modes:** content only for strangers, silence after purchase.

## Referral: others bring others

- **Levers:** shareable carousels and Reels, customer tags, collabs, referral
  codes, UGC reposts, partnership ads.
- **Signals:** shares, tags, branded mentions, codes redeemed.
- **Failure modes:** nothing worth sharing, no ask, no way to track referrals.

## Reading the funnel

| Pattern | Meaning | First move |
|---|---|---|
| High reach, few follows | Content entertains the wrong or unfocused audience | Sharpen the niche in hooks and bio |
| Good follows, few link taps | Weak next step | Rework profile and CTAs |
| Link taps, few sales | Offer or destination problem | Fix the page and proof |
| Sales, no repeat | Retention gap | Customer content and follow-up |
| Few shares | Not share-worthy | More useful or more opinionated content |
