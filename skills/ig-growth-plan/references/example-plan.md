# Example: a short plan for a solo coach

A compact illustration of how a finished plan reads. Replace every number with
the account's real data.

**Account:** a fitness coach for new mothers, 3,200 followers, 5 hours a week.
**Goal:** 12 paying clients in 90 days at a core price of 197 a month.

## 1. Snapshot
Posts 3 Reels a week, answers comments sometimes, no DM system. Profile link
goes to a generic page.

## 2. Positioning
For new mothers who want to rebuild strength without long sessions, against
generic gym programs and YouTube workouts. Promise: 20 minutes, at home, no
equipment.

## 3. Current-state score: 38 of 85
Gaps: DM flow (1), conversion tracking (1), offer clarity (2).

## 4. Funnel
Leakiest stage: activation. Reach is fine. Profile visits do not turn into DMs.

## 5. Content system
Pillars: quick workouts, mother-body education, client results. Series: "20
minutes, no equipment", every Tuesday and Friday. Mix: 3 Reels, 1 carousel, daily
Stories.

## 6. Growth levers
| Lever | Metric | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Comment keyword to DM | DMs started per week | Day 30 |
| Collab with a postnatal physio | Follows per collab | Day 45 |

## 7. Conversion path
Bio rewrite, three pins (result, method, offer), DM keyword "START", booking
link with UTM per post.

## 8. Paid
None until day 60. Then boost the best Reel with a click-to-DM objective.

## 9. Roadmap
- Days 1 to 30: rebuild profile, set DM flow, log leads.
- Days 31 to 60: run the series, start one collab.
- Days 61 to 90: boost the winner, launch a 4-week challenge.

## 10. Measurement
Weekly: reach, follows, DMs, link taps. Monthly: post-mortem. Baseline taken
from the last 30 posts.

## 11. Budget and risks
Time only for 60 days, then a small boost budget. Risks: burnout, health claims
rules, one-format dependence.
