## Data sources

- Instagram insights export (manual, monthly)
- Meta Business Suite and Ads Manager
- Link-in-bio or UTM analytics
- A simple DM and lead log

State what is wired up and what is manual. A plan that assumes data nobody
collects is a plan that fails.
