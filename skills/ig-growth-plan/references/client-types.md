# Account Types

Adjust the plan to the type of account. The structure stays. The weights change.

## Creator or personal brand
- **Goal:** audience, then products or sponsorships.
- **Weights:** series, hooks, face-on-camera consistency, community.
- **Watch:** burnout, over-dependence on one viral format.
- **Revenue:** brand deals, digital products, memberships, affiliate.

## Local business
- **Goal:** foot traffic, bookings, calls.
- **Weights:** location, Stories, Maps and bio, social proof, local creators.
- **Watch:** reaching people outside the service area.
- **Revenue:** bookings and walk-ins. Track with codes and "how did you hear".

## Ecommerce brand
- **Goal:** orders at a target cost.
- **Weights:** product demos, UGC, shop tags, catalog ads, retargeting.
- **Watch:** margin, returns, stock for viral moments.
- **Revenue:** orders. Pixel and Conversions API matter early.

## B2B or service personal brand
- **Goal:** qualified conversations.
- **Weights:** authority content, DMs, case studies, lead magnet, calls.
- **Watch:** Instagram may be the wrong first channel. Test before committing.
- **Revenue:** booked calls and retainers.

## Coach or course creator
- **Goal:** waitlist, launch, evergreen funnel.
- **Weights:** transformation proof, challenges, DMs, launch sequences.
- **Watch:** claims and testimonials rules, refund exposure.
- **Revenue:** programs. Add an email list early.

## Agency managing client accounts
- **Goal:** results and retention of the client.
- **Weights:** approvals workflow, reporting, creative production at scale.
- **Watch:** scope creep, client voice, access and permissions.
- **Deliverables:** monthly plan, creative calendar, report, and a quarterly review.

## Team and agency model

For plans with more than one person, add a roles table: strategy, production
(filming and editing), community, paid, reporting. Name one owner per row and
the weekly hours. If the owner is one person, say so and cut scope to match.

An agency engagement should state the cadence of work: weekly status,
monthly review, quarterly re-plan, and the approval path for every post.
