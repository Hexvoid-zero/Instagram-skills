# Creator Playbook

Detailed formats for `ig-coach`. Keep every recommendation specific to the
creator's audience, evidence and capacity.

## Contents

- Snapshot and diagnosis
- Idea cards
- Script format
- Potential score
- Video review checklist
- Post-publish learning
- Posting and repurposing

## Snapshot and diagnosis

Gather only what is needed: handle, niche or offer, intended viewer, language
and region, goal, style, cadence, and a sample of recent posts. Useful
performance fields are reach, average watch time, completion, likes, comments,
shares, saves, profile visits and follows. Work with what exists and name what is
missing.

Audit:

- **Positioning.** Can a new viewer tell in a few seconds who the account helps
  or entertains, and why to follow?
- **Repeatable strengths.** Which topics, formats, openings or viewer responses
  recur in the stronger posts?
- **Friction.** Where do topic breadth, weak context, slow openings, an unclear
  payoff, inconsistent execution or unsupported claims get in the way?
- **Next move.** One priority action and a simple experiment with a measurable
  outcome and a review window. Avoid "bad niche" or "post more" without showing
  what to change and why.

Compare to the creator's own recent baseline (the median of the last 20 to 30
posts), not a viral benchmark. Separate observed metrics from interpretation.

## Idea cards

For each idea:

1. Working title and intended viewer
2. Why this creator can credibly make it
3. The first spoken line or first-frame text
4. Core sequence and payoff
5. Suggested visual treatment or demonstration
6. Optional CTA and the signal to learn from

Avoid near-duplicates. Mix useful, entertaining, narrative, proof or demo, and
audience-response ideas only where they fit the niche. A viral reference is a
pattern hypothesis, not evidence the new version will repeat it.

## Script format

Use a timing table or short labeled blocks, not long prose. Build the hook and
beat sheet with `/ig-reel`.

- **Concept / viewer / promise**
- **0 to 2 s, hook:** exact spoken line and the visible first frame or action
- **Development:** concise lines, action or B-roll, on-screen text. Each beat
  adds new information.
- **Payoff:** answers the opening or delivers the promised result
- **CTA:** one natural next action, if any (a keyword for a DM, a save, a follow)
- **Caption and tags:** from `/ig-caption`, specific and not stuffed
- **Production notes:** framing, props, cuts, captions, safe zone

Timing is a pacing guide, not a retention guarantee. Use the requested length,
otherwise state the assumed duration. Make the first two seconds legible and
visually active. No curiosity gap the video does not close.

## Potential score

Score 0 to 100 only when there is enough script or concept detail. The weights
are a house heuristic and are not statistically validated.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Audience fit and specific relevance | 20 |
| Hook clarity and curiosity | 20 |
| Retention structure and pacing | 20 |
| Payoff: value or entertainment delivered | 15 |
| Originality and creator fit | 10 |
| Share, save and comment potential | 10 |
| Execution feasibility | 5 |

For each factor give a short evidence-based reason and one practical
improvement. For the hook line, `hookscore.py` in `/ig-reel` gives a second
opinion. Define the score as a comparative creative review, not a chance of going
viral or a projected view count. State confidence (low, medium or high) from the
completeness of the evidence. Do not score with real performance data here. That
belongs in the post-publish review.

## Video review checklist

If a video or transcript is provided, inspect:

- The opening frame and first message: topic and relevance are clear without
  setup
- Whether every line and shot moves the idea forward; flag pauses, repetition,
  jargon and context that arrives late
- Whether visuals show or reinforce the promise, and whether cuts or overlays get
  in the way
- Whether the payoff answers the hook and the CTA matches the goal
- Accessibility: readable captions, contrast, and no essential meaning carried
  only by audio
- Instagram fit: 9:16, text clear of the top and bottom interface bands, a cover
  frame that reads on the grid, music rights for a branded post

Give timestamped notes where possible. Prioritize one major edit, then optional
refinements. Do not infer retention or reaction from a file alone.

## Post-publish learning

Compare the post with the creator's own baseline and, when available, a similar
past post. Do not treat reach as the only outcome:

- Watch time and completion show structure
- Shares and saves show utility and resonance
- Comments show conversation
- Profile visits and follows show audience conversion

These are diagnostic clues, not causal laws. Recommend one next test that changes
a limited number of variables and keeps what already works.

## Posting and repurposing

If analytics show audience-active windows, test around them. If not, pick a
schedule the creator can keep and test different slots. Do not name an exact best
time. Use a few relevant hashtags and search terms spanning the exact topic, with
no live performance ranking claimed. Cross-posting is a user-reviewed
recommendation: prepare an Instagram-specific caption, framing and CTA rather than
reusing another platform's metadata. For turning long content into Reels, use
`/ig-repurpose`.
