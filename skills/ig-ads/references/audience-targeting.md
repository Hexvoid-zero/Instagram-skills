# Audience Targeting for Instagram Ads

Instagram is bought through Meta, so the audience tools are Meta's. The
principle: broad targeting plus specific creative. Use audiences to feed and
protect the algorithm, not to box it in.

## Contents

- Core audiences
- Custom audiences (the ones that matter on Instagram)
- Lookalike audiences
- Advantage+ audience and going broad
- Size guidelines
- Exclusion strategy

## Core audiences (interest and demographic)

- Start broad. Country, age range if the product truly has one, language.
- Interest stacking now hurts more than it helps. If an interest is used at
  all, use one as a signal, not a wall.
- Behavior and life-event targeting have thinned out. Do not build a plan on
  them.
- Always exclude existing customers unless the ad is an upsell.

## Custom audiences

**Instagram-native engagement** (the strongest warm audiences for creators and
small brands):

- People who engaged with the professional account (any interaction, 365 days)
- People who watched a Reel or video at 25%, 50%, 75% or 95%
- People who saved or shared a post
- People who sent a message to the account
- Profile visitors

**Website and shop:**

- All visitors (up to 180 days), key-page visitors, time-on-site thresholds
- Add-to-cart and checkout-start for ecommerce

**Customer list:**

- Upload emails or phone numbers. Match rates run roughly 30 to 70%. Refresh
  regularly.

**Lead form openers and DM conversations** work as audiences too. People who
started a conversation but did not buy are the best retargeting seed an
Instagram account has.

## Lookalike audiences

- Source quality beats source size. Use buyers, not all followers. Purchasers
  beat leads, leads beat engagers, engagers beat visitors.
- Minimum about 100 source people, ideally 1,000+.
- Sizes: 1% most similar, 1 to 3% a good default, 3 to 5% for scale, 5%+ is
  awareness only.
- Exclude the source audience from the lookalike campaign.
- Test lookalike against fully broad once there is spend behind it. Broad often
  wins.

## Advantage+ audience and going broad

Run the simple test: take a winning ad with interest targeting, duplicate it,
remove everything except the country, and run both side by side for seven days
at equal budget. Compare cost per result, not CTR. Broad typically wins when
the creative states who it is for.

## Size guidelines

| Placement set | Minimum | Ideal |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram plus Facebook, country-level | 100,000+ | 500K to 10M |
| Local business, radius or city | 50,000+ | 100K to 1M |
| Lookalike 1% | Source of 1,000+ | n/a |

Too narrow means expensive and slow learning. Too broad with generic creative
means wasted spend. Specific creative on a broad audience solves both.

## Exclusion strategy

Always exclude:

- Existing customers (unless upsell) and recent converters (7 to 14 days)
- People who bounced in under 10 seconds
- The seed audience of any lookalike campaign
- Employees and team accounts
- Retargeting pools that are too small to deliver (under about 1,000 people):
  widen the window instead of paying to hit the same 200 people
